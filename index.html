Zhengyang WAN

I’m a Ph.D. student at University of Wisconsin–Madison, working with Dr. Sikai (Sky) Chen in Sky-Lab. Before that I did my B.E. and M.S. at Tongji University, where I worked on vehicle dynamics and driving simulators for the Virtual Track Train.

My research is about getting autonomous driving to work outside the simulator. I work on sim-to-real transfer — most recently Sim2Real-AD, which runs simulation-trained driving policies on our full-scale test van — and on using vision-language models and world models to make driving decisions safer. I also help build SkyDrive, Sky-Lab’s open-source platform for studying how human drivers, pedestrians and AI interact.
