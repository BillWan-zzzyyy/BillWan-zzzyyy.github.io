I'm a Ph.D. student at <img src="/assets/img/uwmadison.png" class="inline-logo" alt=""/>University of Wisconsin–Madison, working with Dr. [Sikai (Sky) Chen](https://sky-lab-uw.github.io/people/) in <img src="/assets/img/skylab.png" class="inline-logo" alt=""/>[Sky-Lab](https://sky-lab-uw.github.io/). Before that I did my B.E. and M.S. at <img src="/assets/img/tongji.png" class="inline-logo" alt=""/>Tongji University, where I worked on vehicle dynamics and driving simulators for the Virtual Track Train.

My research is about getting autonomous driving to work outside the simulator. I work on sim-to-real transfer, running simulation-trained driving policies on our full-scale test van, and on using vision-language models and world models to make driving decisions safer. I also help build <img src="/assets/img/Sky-Drive.png" class="inline-logo" alt=""/>SkyDrive, Sky-Lab's open-source platform for studying how human drivers, pedestrians and AI interact.
